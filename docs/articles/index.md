# Articles

### All vignettes

- [Checking normality and
  outliers](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/EXPLORE_ASSUMPTIONS.md):

  Check normality and find outliers with rwf: diagnostic plots, eight
  normality tests, three outlier detection rules, and the formulas
  behind them.

- [Recoding and stacking
  data](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/FUNCTIONS_RECODE.md):

  Recode data with rwf: stack a list of data frames, reverse-score
  items, turn multiple-response answers into dummy columns and collapse
  rare factor levels.

- [Basic statistical
  functions](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/FUNCTIONS_STATISTICAL.md):

  Basic statistics with rwf: Bonferroni and Šidák adjustments, standard
  scores, skewness, kurtosis, standard errors and confidence intervals,
  with their formulas.

- [Training and testing
  models](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/FUNCTIONS_TRAIN_TEST.md):

  Evaluate classification models with rwf: ROC curves, confusion
  matrices, separability plots, performance at different cut-offs, and
  k-fold and k-sample cross-validation.

- [ANOVA](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_ANOVA.md):

  ANOVA with rwf: Kruskal-Wallis, one-way and factorial ANOVA, MANOVA,
  effect sizes and post hoc comparisons, next to the results of
  established packages.

- [ANOVA effect
  sizes](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_COMPUTE_AOV_ES.md):

  An ANOVA table with Type I, II or III sums of squares and six effect
  sizes for every factor, from eta-squared to Cohen’s f, with rwf’s
  compute_aov_es.

- [Post hoc
  comparisons](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_COMPUTE_POSTHOC.md):

  Compare every pair of group means after a one-way ANOVA with the
  Tukey-Kramer and Games-Howell tests, with rwf’s compute_posthoc.

- [Correlation](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_CORRELATION.md):

  Correlation with rwf: heatmaps, power analysis, a full report of r, p,
  confidence intervals and adjusted p, and tetrachoric, polychoric,
  biserial and polyserial correlations.

- [Exploratory factor
  analysis](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_EFA.md):

  Exploratory factor analysis with rwf: loadings and scree plots, model
  loadings, residual statistics and a full EFA report.

- [Friedman
  test](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_FRIEDMAN.md):

  The Friedman test, the non-parametric repeated measures ANOVA, with
  Kendall’s W as its effect size and a bootstrap confidence interval,
  with rwf’s compute_friedman_test.

- [Hierarchical linear
  regression](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_HLR.md):

  Hierarchical linear regression with rwf’s report_hlr: add predictors
  in blocks and see how much more variance each block explains, as the
  change in R².

- [Item response theory
  reports](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_IRT.md):

  Item response theory reports with rwf: test information and expected
  score curves, and a full report of a mirt model with its coefficients,
  local independence and fit.

- [Thurstonian item response
  theory](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_IRT_T.md):

  Thurstonian IRT for forced-choice and ranking questionnaires with rwf:
  the comparison design, encoding rank data, ability estimation and MAP
  scoring from a lavaan model.

- [Unidimensional IRT from
  scratch](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_IRT_U.md):

  Unidimensional IRT from scratch in base R with rwf: the item response
  function, item and test information for the 1PL, 2PL and 3PL models,
  and Newton-Raphson ability estimates.

- [Kruskal–Wallis
  test](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_KRUSKAL_WALLIS.md):

  The Kruskal-Wallis test, the non-parametric one-way ANOVA, with two
  rank effect sizes and bootstrap confidence intervals, with rwf’s
  compute_kruskal_wallis_test.

- [Linear
  regression](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_LINEAR_REGRESSION.md):

  Linear regression with rwf: scatterplots and full reports of simple
  and multiple regression models.

- [Logistic
  regression](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_LOGISTIC_REGRESSION.md):

  Logistic regression with rwf: the logistic function, plots of a fitted
  model, likelihood-ratio tests of nested models and a full diagnostic
  report.

- [t tests and Wilcoxon
  tests](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_MEANS.md):

  Pairwise t tests and Wilcoxon tests with rwf’s report_ttests and
  report_wtests, with effect sizes, confidence intervals and
  Bonferroni-adjusted significance.

- [One-way
  ANOVA](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_ONE_WAY.md):

  The one-way ANOVA with Fisher’s or Welch’s F test, five effect sizes
  and the observed power, with rwf’s compute_one_way_test.

- [Plotting means, interactions and ANOVA
  diagnostics](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_PLOT_ANOVA.md):

  Plot group means, interactions and ANOVA diagnostics with rwf, to see
  what the model found and whether its assumptions hold.

- [Reliability and
  validity](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_RELIABILITY.md):

  Reliability and validity with rwf: Cronbach’s alpha and its item
  diagnostics, the multitrait-multimethod (MTMM) matrix and
  Shrout-Fleiss reliability.

- [Structural equation modeling and
  CFA](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/GLM_SEM.md):

  Structural equation modeling and CFA with rwf: path diagrams without
  semPlot, a full CFA report and a simulation of how model fit changes
  with sample size.

- [Natural language
  processing](https://sedzinfo.github.io/rwf/articles/NLP.md):

  Natural language processing with rwf: clean text, remove stopwords,
  tag parts of speech, compare documents and count words and characters.
